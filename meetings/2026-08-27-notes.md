funding downramp
----------------

Daniel: As we move into a period when HPE employees are no longer
driving Chapel development, what is the status of our ability to
support external developers with merge privileges?

Brad: Good, big question.  Historically, the sticking point has been
the testing we run within the HPE firewall on HPC systems and the
security queestion of what it means to have non-HPE contributors doing
merges.  Moving much of the testing to GitHub actions helps with this
situation a lot, but currently we don't have a public alternative to
running on supercomputers like HPE Cray EX / Apollo nor for
performance runs.  If we continue to rely on HPE resources, will have
to figure out how to do this safely.  HPSF core level also requires
this, so it's an important question to wrestle with (in addition to
simply being a better posture for an open-source project to have).
Don't have this topic on today's agenda, but it'd be good for next
time.

TSC meetings
------------
* People generally liked idea of a regular periodicity with the ability to
  trigger a meeting when necessary

* Daniel: If a meeting is triggered, does that reset the clock, or do we
  keep the regularly scheduled meeting as well?

* After some discussion, decided that for now, we'd determine this at
  the meetings themselves, e.g. based on whether the triggered meeting
  wiped the slate clean or only had the chance to focus on the topic
  that triggered it.

* What value of n for "every n months" meetings
  - some support for each of 3, 6, and 4
  - decided having one after funding ends would be good, so discussed
    holding one in Nov or Dec of this year
  - generally decided maybe we could determine n at a given meeting
    based on what was on the horizon, how we were feeling
